How to play

Take turns playing cards or moving units. You have 2 actions per turn. Each card played and each unit activated costs one action. Draw 2 cards at the start of each turn (up to 7 in hand).

01 / Deploy

Place characters on your own edge only: Azure on the left, Ember on the right. Place supplies and terrain on any empty tile. Either army can claim a supply first.

02 / Move & attack

Select your unit, then a glowing hex. Movement and attacking are one action: you occupy the destination if its defender falls. You cannot move onto a living unit or fence.

03 / Win glory

Defeating a unit earns 1 glory (a dragon earns 2). The first time each of your units reaches the far edge earns 2 glory. First to 6 wins.

04 / Special moves

Archers leap exactly 2 hexes, never 1. Rams strike all 3 hexes in a line, then rest through their next turn and take +1 damage while resting. Dragons breathe across four hexes in a forward cone, including friendly units.

Blacksmiths shield an adjacent ally when deployed. Thieves steal a shield when they hit. Fences block movement, brambles hurt entrants, and soup heals them. Shield, potion, poison, extra action, confusion, and double-edged sword supplies trigger when entered.

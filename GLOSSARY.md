# Hex Battle glossary

- **Active character:** A living character on the board owned by the current player. It still counts if it already moved this turn. A battering ram resting after a charge does not count until its rest turn ends.
- **Deployment edge:** The leftmost column for Azure or the rightmost column for Ember. Characters deploy on their own edge; items cannot be placed on either edge.
- **Item:** A supply or terrain card placed on a hex, such as a shield, potion, fence, or bramble. Character cards and Soup of Seeing are not map items.
- **Placement distance:** The minimum number of steps between two hexes through adjacent hexes. A roll of 2–6 allows an item on an empty non-edge hex at exactly that distance from at least one active character; the path does not have to be straight.
- **Committed roll:** Clicking **Roll to place** spends the selected item card and one action. A 1 places the item randomly; a 2–6 requires choosing an eligible hex if one exists. If none exists, or there are no active characters, placement is random on an empty non-edge hex.
- **Hidden item:** An opponent-placed map item. It looks like an empty hex and its placement is omitted from the opponent's battle log. The owner always sees it; a player who discovers a surviving fence by striking it continues to see that fence.
- **Soup of Seeing:** A consumable card used directly on one of your characters that currently has a possible move. It costs a card and an action, not a die roll, and reveals a snapshot of opponent items in that character's possible movement hexes through the end of your turn. A ram's snapshot includes each hex along its possible charge lines. Multiple uses combine.
- **Charge path:** The three consecutive hexes a battering ram strikes in one direction. It enters cleared hexes one at a time and collects their items in order. A surviving obstacle or Confusion stops its travel but not its remaining strikes; a fatal item stops both.
